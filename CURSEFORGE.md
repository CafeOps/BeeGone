# Bee Gone

Tired of the letter B? Bee Gone wipes it out of your messages.

Every B you type in the message box turns into the letter that follows it in the alphabet, the instant you type it. Lines that start with a slash are left alone, so emotes, whispers and other slash tools still work.

Type **/beegone** to turn it on or off. Your setting is saved.

Made for WoW Forever.
